Quick steps to dial in an espresso shot

  1. Set your dose and expected yield using the sliders.
  2. Adjust extraction time to match your machine and watch flow and ratio.
  3. Select the taste profile that best matches the shot you tasted.
  4. Follow the prioritized recommendation — ratio is highlighted first.
  5. Tweak grind, dose, or yield until the shot lands on balanced.
