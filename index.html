How it works

Quick steps to dial in an espresso shot

  1. Set the dose, yield, time, and grind you actually pulled.
  2. Watch ratio and flow as you match the shot on the machine.
  3. Choose the taste that best matches the cup.
  4. Apply one suggestion at a time — ratio first, then grind, then time or yield.
  5. Taste again and repeat until the shot lands on balanced.
